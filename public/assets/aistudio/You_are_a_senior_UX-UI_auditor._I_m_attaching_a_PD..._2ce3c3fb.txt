2. Recurring Patterns Across Pages
1. Light Mode Contrast Regression vs. Dark Mode: Across all 106 states (summarized on PDF pages 107–111), Dark Mode consistently passes contrast checks (except PDF page 52), whereas Light Mode fails color-contrast on 44 out of 53 Light Mode captures because warning badges (DEMO, 1 انتظار), financial debt pills, and muted subtitles use mid-tone orange and light-grey tokens against white/off-white surfaces.
2. Global & Contextual Chrome Crowding Out the Viewport: Both top-level screens (with stacked Backup + Demo banners on PDF pages 7–26, 61–74, 101–106) and MasterPatientChart screens (with 7 stacked header/context rows on PDF pages 31–52) dedicate 20% to 52% of the 844 CSS px vertical height to persistent bars and banners, starving the actual clinical workspace of vertical room and pushing modal submit buttons below the fold (PDF pages 45, 46, 53, 54, 91, 92).
3. Visual Labels Disconnected from Programmatic Accessibility Tree: Visual forms look structured on screen (e.g., SettingsModal: Clinic on PDF pages 79–80 or SessionModal on PDF pages 45–46), but lack <label for> bindings, valid select-name attributes, or matching aria-label text (label-content-name-mismatch), accounting for the majority of the 262 total Axe violations reported on PDF page 107.
4.
